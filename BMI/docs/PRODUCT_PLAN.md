# Product Plan

## Product Positioning

FitCal is a lightweight BMI and calorie tracking app for users who want quick, understandable wellness estimates without account setup or complex nutrition workflows.

The product should feel simple, fast, and practical. It should avoid medical claims and avoid presenting generated guidance as clinical advice.

## App Identity

- App name: FitCal
- Store title: FitCal: BMI & Calorie Tracker
- One-line description: Calculate BMI, estimate daily calories, and get simple diet guidance.
- Category direction: Health & Fitness / Lifestyle utility
- Visual direction: clean, calm, data-focused, international-friendly

## Target Users

- Users who want to check BMI quickly
- Users starting weight loss or maintenance planning
- Users who need a simple daily calorie estimate
- Users who prefer a low-friction app without account login
- Overseas users searching for BMI calculator, calorie calculator, BMR, TDEE, or weight tracker

## Product Principles

- Fast first result: users should get BMI within one screen.
- No medical overclaiming: use wellness language and disclaimers.
- Local-first records: basic use should not require login.
- Simple guidance: clear diet suggestions are better than complex nutrition tables in MVP.
- Ad placement should not interrupt the core calculation flow.

## MVP Scope

The first version should provide a complete offline experience:

- BMI calculation
- Healthy weight range
- BMR and TDEE estimates
- Goal-based calorie target
- Diet guidance templates
- Weight and BMI record history
- Settings for units and privacy/disclaimer access
- Reserved ad slots for result and guidance pages

## Out of Scope for MVP

- Cloud account system
- AI diet generation
- Barcode food scanning
- Social sharing community
- Medical condition-based recommendations
- Wearable device integration

## Success Metrics

- First calculation completion rate
- Result page retention time
- Repeat weight record usage
- Daily active users
- Ad impressions per active user
- Rewarded ad completion rate
- Store conversion from keyword searches

## Long-Term Direction

FitCal can grow from a calculator into a simple weight management companion:

- Trend charts
- Goal tracking
- Meal plan templates
- Reminder system
- Multi-language support
- Premium ad-free option
- More calculators, such as body fat estimate, water intake, macro split, and ideal weight
